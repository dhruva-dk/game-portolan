# Builds, Platform and Testing

Use headless chromium when testing on this mac. Only test on the iPad when necessary due to
the additional build time overhead.
Platform is WebGPU. No WebGL path, no fallbacks, no quality setting, no accessibility options, no older devices.
Target device is M1 iPad at 30 FPS.

# The Game World

No humans, and no sign that people ever lived here.
Textures and recorded sounds come from ambient CG or Poly Haven with all geometry generated in code.
No words, anywhere in the UI.
Numbers are permitted as needed.
Everything the player reads is drawn the way a sixteenth-century chartmaker would draw it. For instance: papyrus and vellum,
iron-gall ink and watercolour, compass roses, rhumb lines, graduated rings, cartouches, pennants.
No scripted events.
No music anywhere.
