# Portolan

Portolan is a naval battle royale for iPhone and iPad on a hand-built ocean planet with no people in it, where the
ships are the characters: they ride a great current across the world, break off into its regions, claim the sea by
sailing loops out of their own waters, and fight to be the last afloat as a storm closes in. It must be beautiful first
and foremost. Every frame a player sees, in every region, at every hour and in every weather, is a cinematic, painterly
3D world that is deep, lit, atmospheric and alive, as rich as the references in `vision/`, never a prototype or a
placeholder, with the menus and the chart in the parchment-and-ink identity of a portolan chart. It must play as well
as it looks: every match has a rhythm of calm, tension and payoff, the controls feel immediate and weighty under a
thumb, the rival captains are worth outwitting, and the player always understands why it won or lost and wants another
match.

It runs on iPhone 15 and newer and iPads with an M1 or newer, in landscape and by touch, offline, at a steady 30 fps
through a whole match at native resolution, with a build under 1 GB. It is TypeScript, three.js and Vite, wrapped for
iOS with Capacitor. All geometry is generated in code; textures, fonts, sounds and music come only from the
highest-quality sources, each credited in `CREDITS.md`.
