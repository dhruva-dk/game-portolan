# Decisions

What the owner has decided for the manager's game (branch `mm`). Quoted lines
are the owner's own words. A line marked "asked" was a question put to the
owner and answered yes. Nothing else is binding: no bands, claims or targets
that an agent wrote for itself.

## The goal

- "the goal is to clone mm's ui in our kit. so if mm uses a table somewhere,
  we are ok to use a table. but regressions to tables are prohibited"
- "make the UI in style more like our current game while making sure that each
  screen actually follows the MM screen"
- The MM screens are "much more graphic and immersive"; ours being "just
  spreadsheets" is a failure.

## The UI (22 Nov, asked and answered yes)

1. One hand. The flat office kit goes. Every office panel, button, strip and
   nav is drawn in the race HUD's hand (rough-cut paper, double rule, ink,
   italic figures), with no navy bars. MM's positions and content stay where MM
   has them. The pieces added to the race (standings tower, team-mate cards,
   Orders, Pit Stop) get the same treatment.
2. Ship and Sponsors show the live 3D ship through a clear window, with MM's
   panels round it.
3. HQ is the harbour in actual 3D, each building labelled and tapped to open
   its card, MM's right-hand panel beside it. (The inked chart of woodcuts
   first agreed was rejected on sight: "why is screenshot 3 just some shitty
   svgs instead of an actual 3d environment".)
4. Calendar follows MM's three columns, each race's planet standing in for the
   circuit picture.
5. Tables only where MM has tables (Scouting, Standings, Finances, the results
   table). Drivers, Staff, Mail, Team and Part Design are MM's cards.
6. Home, Ship and HQ are redone first. No screen is done until its capture has
   been shown to the owner beside its MM reference.

## Pictures (22 Nov)

- No drawn stand-ins for what MM shows as a picture: "why is the top right
  literally just a compass rose", "some shitty svg of our ship for no reason
  that provides no value at all". Pictures come from the game's own 3D.
- "if MM has some element that is not possible to replicate well in ours,
  scrap it rather than making a bad version ... but you need to tell me for
  those"

## Not to spend time on

- Cross-machine hash comparison: "why are we wasting so much time comparing
  hashes of things".
- Subagents: "we are burning through usage too fast. just do all work
  yourself".
