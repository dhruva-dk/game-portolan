Portolan UI: before and after

Old game (0117) drawn from its chartLayout numbers (iPhone, 956 x 440, safe 62 / 62 / 0 / 21) against the new UI drawn from src/ui. Backdrops are flat; marks are the real paths and inks. Old tab, ability and reload icons are stand-ins from the shared woodcut set.

01Title

  • Same sheet, rose, needle and gilt galleon; rose 2 px smaller and 8 px higher (R126 at y182 vs R128).
  • Dropped: the web inking itself in, the sheet thinning to show the world, the rose flying to the lobby plate.
  • Added: needle overshoot when the world is ready; tap ignored until ready.

02Lobby

  • Dropped: the four tabs (abilities, locker, rank, rivals), bottom left, r23 at 58 px steps.
  • Dropped: orbit and pinch of the ship, the folio sheet, the rose landing on the ring.
  • Resized: career plate 110 to 132 wide; wheel r24 to r26; PLAY banner kept at its spot (about 210 x 94).
  • Moved: rank folio now opens from the plate; plate nudged 10 px left, wheel 8 px right.

03Abilities folio

  • Cut: 8 active and 8 passive woodcut cards, worn one sealed, scroll window 455 x 256, 212 pt cards.
  • No ability button on the new HUD either (old one: r31 at 702,259).

04Locker folio

  • Cut: six cosmetic groups (sail colour and pattern, hull paint, figurehead, emblem, wake), four 100 pt cards a row.

05Rivals folio

  • Cut: roster of 19 bot captains with a head-to-head record each, two cards a row at 214 x 133.

06Rank

  • Dropped: the staff of eight pennants, matches sailed and wins numerals, the cosmetics each tier brings, scrolling.
  • Added: rank seal and level ring drawn large side by side, all eight tiers in one row with a gilt marker on hers; it all fits without scrolling.
  • Moved: from a right-docked scrolling sheet (x347, 535 wide) to a centred folio 496 x 330 at (230,50); close by tapping outside.
  • Career values are placeholder (level 7, tier 2).

07Settings

  • Dropped: 8 of 10 controls: stick side, haptics, shake, effects/ambience/interface volume split, invert look, auto-fire; left-handed mode goes with them.
  • Kept: sound (speaker, azure bar) and look speed (sight, ochre bar), set by a tap along the bar, saved on the device.
  • Moved/resized: from a 535 x 335 right-docked sheet to a small 332 x 168 folio at (560,74); bars 111 to 200 wide.

08Gale drop

  • Dropped: the DIVE slip (128 x 102 at 802,317, under the right thumb), the Gale course dotted on the disc, the glide stick and pennant marker, the first-drop hint.
  • Added: the whole match HUD is up during the drop; a 10 s countdown on a 64 x 56 slip (centre, y52) ends by itself, so there is no choice of when to go.
  • Moved: countdown from y12 to y52, below the clock.

09Match HUD

  • Dropped: the separate Fire button (r40 at 802,317, right thumb home). The new HUD has none yet; a fire button is being added on the right beside gust.
  • Dropped: ability (r31 at 702,259), reload (r28 at 690,347), sails lever (r28 at 160,207), reticle (r10 at centre), hail seal (r25 at 704,131) and coin tally (104 x 28), hurts row, hit figures, incoming-fire arcs.
  • Moved: gust from r28 at (808,231), above fire, to a 60 px disc on the right edge at (860,232); hotbar from 5 slots centred at x436 to 4 slots starting at x492 (cannon, shield, kit, empty), chosen slot gilt.
  • Resized: vitals bars 150 to 158 wide and 18 to 15 tall inside one 214 x 60 slip; minimap disc r75 to r66 and moved from (809,85) to (824,70); clock and afloat slips 84 to 88 wide, y12 to y4.
  • All new values are placeholders (hull 100, shield 40, gust 2, fixed slots).

10Full map

  • Dropped: live 3D planet seen through a ~430 px window (now a drawn orthographic globe, R202 at centre), pinch zoom, the marker pennant, other ships by emblem, kills and damage rows, the minimap disc as a second close target.
  • Added: 30 degree graticule, storm wash and swirls outside the calm, rubric calm edge, her gilt galleon.
  • Moved: readings from four rows down the right (kills, damage, ships, clock) to one 120 x 110 slip with two (ships, clock); drag spins, a tap closes.

11Storm

  • Before: red rim wash round the screen edge while outside the circle (ellipse 62% to edge), clock figure and spiral in rubric while it closes.
  • After: the HUD has no storm state beyond the clock going red under 30 s; the full-map wash is built. The world look (rain, cloud, veil) is built in sky/storm.ts but has no UI.
  • Not built: rim wash, direction-to-safety mark, outside-circle damage readout.

12Spectate

  • Before: after sinking only the Storm clock (390,12) and the disc, rimmed gilt, remain; a tap anywhere skips to the result.
  • After: no spectate state in flow.ts; the match end goes straight to the result.

13Victory

  • Before: no UI at all; the HUD dries away and the camera draws back and circles her; tap or wait 6 s.
  • After: no victory state; a win goes straight to the result sheet with the gold laurel.

14Result

  • Dropped: ships sunk and damage dealt (old row of three at y273, x320/478/636), the watercolour chart of the water held, level ticking and seal strike, the 0.8 s tap guard.
  • Moved: laurel from centre (478,132) r95 to (560,150) r82; round medallion left now holds her galleon, not a chart; plate from a small 80 wide at the bottom to 144 wide, bottom centre-left.
  • Added: time afloat beside the laurel (uses the ships-left glyph, so it can read as a ship count); a tap returns to the lobby. Career does not change after a match (placeholder).
