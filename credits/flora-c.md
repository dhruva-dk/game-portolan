# Credits: Flora C (the Glass Sea, the Jade Shoals, the Coral Atolls, the Spice Isles)

Every plant and coral is generated in code, and so are the painted textures (palm fronds, fan leaves, grass heads,
flowers, the sea fan's lattice). The scanned textures below live in `public/tex/flora/c/`, resized to 1024 px and
re-encoded as JPG; the leaf sets' photo backgrounds were filled with the leaves' average colour.
